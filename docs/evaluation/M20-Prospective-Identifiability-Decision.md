# M20 Prospective Identifiability Decision

## Decision

**PROSPECTIVE DIAGNOSTIC CONTRACT FEASIBLE.**

The current evaluator cannot retrospectively identify whether a v5 evaluator
failure arose from payload accuracy, public prerequisites, or both. A narrowly
scoped, evaluator-private diagnostic schema can make those categories
prospectively observable while preserving success decisions and provider
isolation.

## Authorization boundary

This decision authorizes no implementation, manifest, live provider request,
calibration rerun, pilot, formal evaluation, or new generation. A future study
requires a separate implementation issue, privacy and persistence validation,
fresh frozen identity, and independent authorization.
