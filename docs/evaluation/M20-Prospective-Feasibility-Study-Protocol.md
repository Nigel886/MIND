# M20 Prospective Task/Evaluator Feasibility Study Protocol

## Scope

Protocol identity: `m20_task_evaluator_feasibility_v1`. This is a future,
descriptive evaluator/task feasibility study, not M20 confirmatory evaluation.
No execution is authorized here.

## Research questions

- RQ1: task-contract feasibility under one bounded reference policy.
- RQ2: H1 payload, H2 prerequisite, H3 combined, and H4 unknown/other
  identifiability at evaluator handoff.
- RQ3: descriptive failure-stage, incomplete, and resource distributions.
- RQ4: diagnostic noninterference with evaluator outcome and public/provider
  boundaries.

## Population, condition, and sample freeze

Population is all 12 eligible `m20_real_case_source_v1` cases: two cases in
each of six frozen cohorts. The single reference condition is
`m20_mind_fixed_v1`; all summaries are descriptive, never comparative. Freeze
two repetitions per case: 24 original works and 12 case/repetition units. No
adaptive expansion is allowed. Provider/infrastructure failure alone may have
one canonical first replacement; performance outcomes never rerun.

Use `m20_real_ceiling_v2`: 16 reasoning steps, 8 tool attempts, 8 logical
provider interactions, and 8 decision cycles. Stop after all originals and any
eligible first replacements are terminally accounted for, or fail closed.

## Analysis and criteria

Report descriptive success, H1/H2/H3/H4, unknown, incomplete, failure-stage,
resource, and case/cohort-heterogeneity counts only. No p-values, formal N,
or confirmatory effect is permitted. Feasibility requires canonical integrity,
zero diagnostic leakage, unchanged evaluator outcome, accounted work, and a
reported diagnostic completeness/unknown rate. Negative results are preserved
without expansion, task selection, or retrospective design change.
